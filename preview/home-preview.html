The profile

Big shows look effortless when the operations behind them are exact.

That is the work: schedules, contractors, compliance and a thousand moving parts resolved before doors open. I am a Senior Operations Manager at Media 10, leading operations for UK Construction Week across the NEC in Birmingham and ExCeL London, and working alongside the commercial team so that every stand and sponsorship sold is one we can build. Before Media 10 I delivered Gastech with DMG Events and international exhibitions across logistics, fashion and retail with Hyve Group.
